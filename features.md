# Reddit-Like Platform Features

## Must Have

| Feature               | User capability                                                       | Implementation complexity | Priority justification                                        |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Accounts and profiles | Sign up, sign in, manage a profile, and update a password.            | Moderate                  | Required to attribute content, votes, and moderation actions. |
| Communities           | Create, discover, join, and browse topic-based communities.           | Moderate                  | Core organizational unit of the platform.                     |
| Posts and comments    | Publish text, link, or media posts and reply in threaded discussions. | Complex                   | The primary content and conversation loop.                    |
| Voting                | Upvote or downvote posts and comments and see their scores.           | Moderate                  | Enables community-driven content ranking.                     |
| Feeds and sorting     | Browse community and home feeds by Hot, New, and Top.                 | Moderate                  | Makes growing content navigable.                              |
| Search                | Find communities, users, and posts by keywords.                       | Moderate                  | Essential for content discovery and retrieval.                |
| Basic moderation      | Community owners remove content, ban users, and configure rules.      | Complex                   | Needed to keep communities usable and safe.                   |
| Reporting             | Report abusive content or users.                                      | Moderate                  | Provides a safety path for users and moderators.              |

## Should Have

| Feature                 | User capability                                                      | Implementation complexity | Priority justification                                   |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rich post editor        | Format text, attach media, preview links, and save drafts.           | Moderate                  | Improves publishing quality and reduces abandoned posts. |
| Notifications           | Receive alerts for replies, mentions, votes, and moderation actions. | Moderate                  | Brings users back into active discussions.               |
| Saved posts and history | Save content and revisit viewed or submitted posts.                  | Simple                    | Supports research and repeat engagement.                 |
| User reputation         | Earn visible karma or reputation from community participation.       | Moderate                  | Encourages useful contributions.                         |
| Community roles         | Assign moderators, approved contributors, and custom permissions.    | Complex                   | Makes larger communities manageable.                     |
| Flair and tags          | Add user flair, post flair, and filters.                             | Moderate                  | Improves identity and content scanning.                  |
| Direct messaging        | Privately contact other users with blocking controls.                | Complex                   | Adds social connection, with meaningful safety work.     |
| Content filters         | Mute communities, hide spoilers, and filter sensitive content.       | Moderate                  | Gives users control over their experience.               |
